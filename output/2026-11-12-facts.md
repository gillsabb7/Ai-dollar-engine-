## Five detailed facts

### Fact 1: Octopus ke teen dil hote hain
Do dil khoon ko gills tak bhejte hain jahan oxygen milti hai. Teesra dil oxygen wala khoon baaki body tak pahunchata hai. Swimming ke waqt teesre dil ki dhadkan dheemi ho sakti hai.

### Fact 2: Octopus ka khoon neela hota hai
Iske khoon mein hemocyanin naam ka copper-based protein hota hai. Yeh oxygen transport karta hai aur oxygen milne par neela rang dikhata hai.

### Fact 3: Venus par ek din uske saal se lamba hai
Venus ko apni dhuri par ek rotation poora karne mein lagbhag 243 Earth days lagte hain. Suraj ka ek chakkar lagane mein lagbhag 225 Earth days lagte hain.

### Fact 4: Sharks bahut pracheen hain
Sharks ke purvajon ke fossils lagbhag 400 million saal purane milte hain. Isliye sharks ka evolutionary group pedon se bhi purana hai.

### Fact 5: Space ke vacuum mein awaaz travel nahi karti
Awaaz ko hawa, paani ya kisi solid jaise medium ki zaroorat hoti hai. Space ka vacuum aam sound waves ko travel karne ke liye medium nahi deta.
