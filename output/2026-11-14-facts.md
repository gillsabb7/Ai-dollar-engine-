## Five detailed facts

### Fact 1: Sunlight ko Earth tak aane mein 8 minute lagte hain
Sunlight ko Earth tak pahunchne mein average lagbhag 8 minute 20 seconds lagte hain. Isliye hum Sun ko kuch minute purana dekhte hain.

### Fact 2: Plants photosynthesis se food banate hain
Plants sunlight ki energy se carbon dioxide aur water ka use karke sugars banate hain. Is process mein oxygen bhi release hoti hai.

### Fact 3: Saturn ki average density paani se kam hai
Saturn ki average density water se kam hai, lekin iska matlab yeh nahi ki use kisi real ocean mein tairaya ja sakta hai. Aisa ocean maujood nahi.

### Fact 4: Koala fingerprints insaan jaise lag sakte hain
Koala ke fingerprints ka pattern human fingerprints jaisa ho sakta hai. Close examination se dono ko alag pehchana ja sakta hai.

### Fact 5: Bees flowers se nectar collect karti hain
Worker honeybees flowers se nectar leti hain. Hive mein nectar process hota hai aur paani kam hone par honey ke roop mein store kiya jata hai.
