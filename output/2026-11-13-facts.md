## Five detailed facts

### Fact 1: Honey bahut lambe samay tak tik sakta hai
Sahi tarah sealed aur dry rakha honey bahut lambe samay tak kharab hone se bach sakta hai. Iski kam moisture aur acidity kai microbes ki growth ko rokne mein madad karti hai.

### Fact 2: Butterflies pairon se taste karti hain
Butterflies ke pairon par taste-sensing receptors hote hain. Inse woh patton aur plants ko pehchanne mein madad leti hain.

### Fact 3: Banana botanical berry hai
Botany ki definition ke mutabik banana berry hai, kyunki yeh flower ke ovary se develop hone wala fleshy fruit hai. Strawberry is definition mein true berry nahi hai.

### Fact 4: Kuch jellyfish roshni bana sakti hain
Kuch species bioluminescence dikhati hain. Unke body ke chemicals reaction karke light banate hain, jo defence ya communication mein kaam aa sakti hai.

### Fact 5: Skin human body ka sabse bada organ hai
Skin body ko cover karke germs, chot aur paani ke loss se bachati hai. Yeh temperature control aur touch sensation mein bhi madad karti hai.
